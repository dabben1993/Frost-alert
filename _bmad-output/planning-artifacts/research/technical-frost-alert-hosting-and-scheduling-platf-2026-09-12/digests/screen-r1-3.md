### Leads
- Cronhub (explicitly named in the brief) was not investigated this session due to budget — its free-tier terms and reliability reputation are unknown.
- The dev.to comparison piece names "Cronhooks" and "Runhooks" as free-tier alternatives with real retry/webhook support (unlike cron-job.org) — worth a primary-source check if more retry robustness is wanted, but this is currently sourced only from a low-confidence aggregator.
- Pricing conflict resolved: a third-party aggregator (latest.sh) and a dev.to article both cited EasyCron's cheapest paid tier as ~$12/mo, but EasyCron's own live pricing page shows the cheapest paid tier is $24/year (~$2/mo) — the primary source should be trusted over the aggregators.
- Google Cloud Scheduler (~$0.10/job/month) and AWS EventBridge Scheduler (~$1/million invocations) surfaced as "cheap but not free" cloud-native options outside the original candidate list — not a fit for a strict $0 requirement but worth flagging as a fallback if free options prove unreliable.
- No independent (non-self-reported) uptime/incident-history source for cron-job.org was found; the one status-tracking site located had too sparse a check history (19 checks over 900 days) to be meaningful and was excluded from claims.

### Could not find
- Cronhub's free-tier terms and reliability reputation (not searched — budget).
- Independent, dated (≤12 months) user reports of specifically cron-job.org missing/delaying executions (only found reports about GitHub Actions' own scheduler being unreliable, which drove users to cron-job.org).
- Confirmation either way of whether Vercel, Render, Supabase, or Deno Deploy have had a major free-tier cut or deprecation in the past ~2 years (no evidence of cuts found, but this was not exhaustively searched for each platform — absence of evidence is not evidence of absence here).
- A precise, sourced execution-count cap for healthchecks.io's free plan directly from healthchecks.io's own pricing page (the "20 checks" figure came from a third-party Medium post, not confirmed against the official site this session).
