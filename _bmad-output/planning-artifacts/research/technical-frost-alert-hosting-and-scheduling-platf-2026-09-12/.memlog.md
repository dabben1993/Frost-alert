- (decision) Dimension 2 (timing precision, reliability, implementation reality) complete, stopped at round 1 (coverage). Key finding: all 4 finalists have documented reliability gaps. Ranked risk for this use case: Supabase (worst fit, auto-pause conflicts with seasonal design) > GitHub Actions (worst/worsening delay, but best alerting+widest language choice) > Cloudflare (binary failure mode, active incident during research) > Deno Deploy (most controllable via backoffSchedule, but young platform + registration bug). Recommendation carried to verdict: layer an independent dead-man's-switch watchdog regardless of primary scheduler choice.
