Frost Alert for Overwintering Bonsai

Forge session outcome — Idea: HARDENED

Outcome

  • Started as a half-formed idea ("notify me before it freezes") with one hard constraint stated up front (no phone app) that turned out to be softer than it first appeared, and several hidden forks (scope, trigger model, acknowledgment semantics, hosting) that could each have quietly broken the build later.
  • Ended with a scoped, buildable shape: a personal, config-driven automation with a forecast-based escalating alert model, a seasonal acknowledgment toggle, and hosting/channel requirements clear enough to hand to research.

Locked decisions

  • Scope: Personal automation, easy config for others — not a hosted multi-user service.
  • Constraint, corrected: "No app" means no custom-built native app — installing an existing app (WhatsApp, Telegram, Pushover…) to receive alerts is fine.
  • Trigger: Forecast-based, not live-reading-based, with escalating re-checks as a cold night approaches (24h → 12h → shorter).
  • Acknowledgment: "Moved to overwintering" is a one-time seasonal toggle, not a nightly snooze.
  • Hosting: Must not depend on a personal always-on device; run on a $0 (or cheapest-viable) scheduler.
  • Threshold: User-set number, not hardcoded to 0°C — default carries a safety margin for forecast error and radiative frost.

Rejected along the way

  • Hosted multi-user product — explicitly not wanted; no accounts, no handling others' data.
  • Native Android/macOS app as the delivery mechanism — not a device restriction, a build-effort decision.
  • Live-temperature trigger — gives no lead time to act.
  • Nightly snooze acknowledgment — doesn't match how bonsai overwintering actually works.
  • Hardcoded 0°C threshold — real risk starts above literal freezing.

Weak points that survived scrutiny

  • Escalation cadence: exact steps between 24h and the final warning are still undecided.
  • Channel shortlist: narrowed by the two-way-acknowledgment requirement, but not yet chosen.
  • Location input: how a friend specifies "where I am" is still open.
  • Message content: what the alert actually says was flagged by the user as unresolved.
